Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers the Community Manager Toolkit by CommonOS: the plugin’s skills, the optional hosted connector, this website, and the support messages you send us.

The publisher is CommonOS, a company being formed in the United Kingdom. Until CommonOS Ltd is incorporated, the toolkit is operated by Jason Basel, an individual based in the UK, who is the controller of the personal data this policy describes. Contact: jason@commonos.ai. In this policy, “we” means CommonOS and, until it is incorporated, Jason Basel.

The short version

  • The skills are instructions only. They have no server, and they do not collect or store anything.
  • The optional connector processes the data your assistant sends it in memory to compute a result, then discards it. It stores nothing, logs no content, has no sign-in and uses no analytics.
  • This website uses no analytics and sets no cookies.
  • The member data you use is yours. If you use it with the toolkit, you are responsible for having a lawful basis and any consent needed.

The plugin’s skills

The skills are a set of written instructions that your AI assistant follows (for example in Claude or ChatGPT). They are not software that runs on a server. We do not receive your prompts, your files, your member data or your outputs, and we cannot see how you use them.

When you use the skills, the information you give your assistant (for example a guest list, a member export or a WhatsApp chat export) and the tools you have connected (for example Luma, HubSpot, Notion, Slack, Google Drive or Airtable) are handled by those providers under their own terms and privacy policies. Check the settings and policies of the assistant and connectors you use.

The skills tell the assistant to read only the fields it needs (such as name, organisation, role, location, what someone wants and offers, and when they were last active), to keep phone numbers and email addresses out of reports, the intro ledger and the member directory, to write drafts rather than send messages, and to ask you before writing anything to a connected tool.

The optional hosted connector

The connector is a hosted service at https://toolkit.commonos.ai/mcp. It gives the skills seven tools: mapping the columns of an export, merging member records, pairing event attendees, segmenting members by activity, planning the week, computing impact figures and checking draft messages. Adding it is your choice. Every skill works without it.

  • What it receives. Whatever your assistant passes to a tool: for example a guest list, member records (names, organisations, roles, locations, what people ask for and offer, last active dates, opt-in status), intro ledger rows or a draft message. Some tools can take an email address so they can recognise the same person in two sources.
  • What it does with it. It processes the data in memory to compute the result, returns the result to your assistant and discards the data. It does not store the data, write it to a database or file, or keep a copy.
  • What it does not return. Its results do not include email addresses or phone numbers. An email address is used only to recognise duplicates, in memory, and is never returned.
  • What it logs. Only the tool name, how long it took, how many records it handled and whether it succeeded. The logs hold no names, message text or other member data. Our host also keeps standard request records (IP address, browser or client identifier, time, address requested and status) to run and secure the service, for up to 30 days by default. They never include what you send to the tools.
  • What it does not do. It has no sign-in or accounts. It uses no cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party scripts. It does not send your data to any other service. Its tools only return a result: they cannot send messages or read or write your other tools.
  • Abuse limits. To protect the service we count requests per IP address in memory and refuse requests that are too large or too frequent. These counts are not written to disk or to the logs.
  • Where it runs. On Google Cloud, in the London region (europe-west2), over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Google Cloud is our hosting provider.

Your assistant and the other tools you connect handle the same data under their own terms. The connector does not change that.

Your responsibilities when you use member data

If you use the toolkit with information about your members or guests, you (or your organisation) are the controller of that data. You decide why and how it is used, and you are responsible for complying with data protection law, including the UK GDPR and GDPR where they apply. This is true whether the data goes to your assistant, to a tool you connect or to the connector. We process what the connector receives only to run the tool you called, and for nothing else. In particular:

  • have a lawful basis for using the data in this way, and get consent where it is needed, for example before contacting people or sharing their details with another member;
  • tell your members how you use their data;
  • do not send more personal data than you need, and avoid special-category data (such as health, religion, ethnicity or sexual orientation). The toolkit is not designed for it, and its skills are written not to infer it;
  • honour removal, opt-out and access requests from your members. The skills ask the assistant to treat them immediately and record them in your intro ledger, but the obligation is yours. The connector stores nothing, so removal happens in your own systems and your assistant’s history.

The consent-first rules in the skills are designed to help, but they do not replace your own legal obligations or advice.

This website

This site is a set of static pages served by the same hosted service as the connector. We run no analytics and set no cookies on it, and it loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. It uses the fonts already on your device. As described above, the host may keep standard request records, such as IP address, time and status.

The source code and issue tracker are on GitHub, which has its own privacy statement.

Support messages and GitHub issues

  • Email. If you email us, we process your email address and what you write in order to reply, because it is in our legitimate interests (and yours) to answer your question. We keep messages only as long as needed to deal with your request.
  • GitHub issues. Issues and comments on GitHub are public and are handled by GitHub. Please do not post member data, personal data or anything confidential there.

We use the host’s request records and the in-memory abuse limits to keep the service running and secure, which is also in our legitimate interests. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. In some cases you can also ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to it. The only personal data we hold is what you send us in support messages, plus the host’s standard request records. The connector stores nothing, so we cannot look up or return data about a person from it. To make a request, or to ask for help or removal, email jason@commonos.ai.

If you are a member of a community whose manager uses the toolkit, send requests about your data to that community manager, since they decide how your data is used.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The toolkit and this site are aimed at people who run communities and are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
