Support

Something not working, or a skill that could be better? Here is how to get help.

Get help

  • GitHub Issues (preferred): https://github.com/jasonbasel/community-manager-toolkit/issues. Bugs, questions and ideas are all welcome. Issues are public.
  • Email: jason@commonos.ai for anything you would rather not post publicly.

This is a free, open-source project run by one person. We answer as quickly as we can, but there is no service-level guarantee.

What to include

  • Which assistant you use (Claude or ChatGPT) and which skill you were using.
  • What you asked, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • Which connectors, if any, were connected, or whether you uploaded a file.
  • If you use the Community Manager Toolkit connector: the tool name, roughly when it ran, and the error text.
Please do not post member data. Do not include names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal details of your members or guests in an issue or email. Describe the problem with made-up examples instead.

Privacy, removal and data requests

  • The skills hold no data, and the connector stores nothing, so there is nothing for us to remove from either. See the privacy policy.
  • To ask about the little we do hold (support emails), or to have it deleted, email jason@commonos.ai.
  • If you are a member of a community and want something you shared removed, or want to opt out of introductions or messages, contact the community manager who runs that community. They decide how your data is used.
  • If you are a community manager and a member asks you to forget something, honour it straight away and note it in your intro ledger. The skills do the same. Removal happens in your own systems and your assistant’s history, because nothing is kept for you elsewhere.

Common questions

A skill did not start. What should I try?

Ask in plain words that match the job, for example “Match the attendees of my next Luma event” or “Help me introduce two members”. Check the plugin is installed and enabled in your assistant.

It cannot see my Luma, Notion or HubSpot data.

Connectors are optional and set up in your assistant’s connector settings. Without one, upload a CSV export and the skill will work from the file.

Can it send the messages for me?

It drafts messages. It sends only if you explicitly ask it to, through a connected tool that asks you to confirm, and never in bulk. The Community Manager Toolkit connector cannot send anything. Most people copy the drafts into WhatsApp or email.

How do I add the connector?

Add https://toolkit.commonos.ai/mcp from your assistant’s connector settings. There is no sign-in. The steps differ by assistant, and the skills work without it.

The connector is slow, refuses a request or returns an error.

It is free and has limits on request size and on how many requests one address can make a minute. Try again in a minute, or send a smaller list. If it keeps failing, open an issue with the tool name and the time. Do not include member data.

I found a security problem.

Email jason@commonos.ai rather than opening a public issue.
