# Community profile: Northbridge Founders Network

Updated: 2026-09-28. Written with the Community Manager Toolkit. Edit any line; the toolkit reads this file first.

## The community

- **Name:** Northbridge Founders Network (we say "Northbridge")
- **What it is:** an invite-only professional network for founders, operators and investors, mostly UK-based
- **Size:** about 200 people. 60 have a full profile in the member directory so far; the rest are being added as they join or reply.
- **What we call people:** members (never "users" or "customers"). Non-members who come to an event are "guests".
- **Run by:** Priya, community manager (one person, part-time)
- **Language:** UK English

## Where things live

- **Member directory:** members.csv (a Google Sheet that I export to CSV for the toolkit)
- **Events:** Luma. I copy the next few into events_upcoming.csv.
- **Intro ledger:** ledger.csv. A CSV that I download and re-upload each time.
- **Sponsors:** Brindlewood Business Bank (main sponsor). I name the contact in each request.

## How we message people

- **Members:** WhatsApp, one to one. Plain text only, 2 to 4 short lines, under about 500 characters, one question per message.
- **Sponsors:** email. A subject line, 3 to 5 short lines, greeting by first name.
- **Guests and applicants:** email only, and only about the event or application they asked about.
- **Voice:** warm and brief. First names, British spelling, no exclamation marks, no jargon, at most one emoji in a message and never in the first line.
- **Sign-off, on every channel:** — Priya, Northbridge

## Who can be messaged

- Every member agreed to WhatsApp messages from Northbridge when they joined.
- Anyone who asks to stop is never contacted again. I record it in the ledger notes as "opt-out".
- Nobody's details go to another member until they have said yes.

## Ground rules for the toolkit

- Draft only. I send everything myself.
- Never pair two people from the same organisation.
