# Community Manager Toolkit

Introductions, event matchmaking and impact reports for community managers, built from
what worked in a real ~850-member professional network.

For anyone running a professional network, association, alumni group, members' club,
accelerator cohort, event series or WhatsApp community who needs to create useful
connections between members and prove the community's value.

## Skills

- **Event matchmaking:** pair attendees from a Luma or Eventbrite guest list with a
  reason and a backup for everyone, plus a host sheet, "find [name]" messages and a
  morning-after follow-up.
- **Member introductions:** consent-first, double opt-in warm intros, with a sales-pitch
  screen, identity checks and follow-ups until you know the conversation happened.
- **Community impact report:** a board or sponsor report on community ROI built on
  outcomes, not activity, with data coverage stated honestly.
- **New member onboarding:** a first month built around one named introduction instead
  of a feature tour, with day-3, day-14 and day-30 check-ins.
- **Member re-engagement:** segment quiet and dormant members, pick the right touch for
  each, and draft a small batch of personal messages instead of a broadcast.

## How it works

Ask in plain words, for example "Match the attendees of my next Luma event" or "Help me
introduce two members, double opt-in". Each skill asks for what it needs in one short
message, shows you a table to approve, then drafts. You approve every pair and every
message. Nothing is sent unless you explicitly ask, one message at a time, and nothing
is ever bulk-sent.

Every introduction goes into an intro ledger (Notion, Google Sheets, Airtable or a CSV
file) with next-check dates, so follow-ups happen and your impact report counts real
outcomes.

Built-in guardrails: no pairs from the same organisation, identity checks for similar
names, no sensitive attributes in match reasons, cooldowns and message spacing for
member engagement, and immediate handling of opt-out and removal requests.

## Connectors (all optional)

Luma, HubSpot, Notion, Slack and Google Drive are pre-configured. Airtable, Gmail,
Circle, Ticket Tailor and RSVPify are used if you have connected them. No connectors?
Upload CSV exports (including Eventbrite attendee reports) or a WhatsApp chat export.
See [CONNECTORS.md](CONNECTORS.md).

## What data goes where

This plugin has no server and stores nothing. Your data stays in your AI assistant and
the tools you connect. The plugin contacts no service other than the connectors you
choose to connect, and it writes to them (ledger rows, drafts, a saved report) only
after you approve. The skills read only the fields they need (name, organisation, role,
location, what people want and offer, last active), never put phone numbers or email
addresses in reports or the ledger, and name members in reports only with your
confirmation.
